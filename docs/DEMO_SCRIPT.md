# PawArt 90-second demo

## Before recording

- Use a physical phone with Expo Go and microphone access enabled.
- Put one clear dog photo in the phone gallery.
- Have a 3–10 second dog bark ready nearby or on a second device.
- Generate one backup artwork in advance in case venue Wi-Fi is slow.

## Walkthrough

1. **Hook (10s):** “Dogs cannot choose their portrait style, so PawArt lets their bark choose it for them.”
2. **Photo (10s):** Add the dog's photo and name.
3. **Bark (15s):** Record a bark while calling out the live waveform and 10-second guardrail.
4. **Trust (10s):** Point to the disclaimer: PawArt measures sound; it does not pretend to translate emotion.
5. **Generation (15s):** Call out the custom progress experience and Google AI architecture.
6. **Reveal (20s):** Show the portrait, acoustic traits, and bark-driven descriptors. Replay the bark.
7. **Proof (10s):** Show a second result from a different bark to demonstrate visibly different color, movement, and contrast.

## Judge-friendly close

“PawArt makes Google AI part of the creative mechanism: Gemini hears the bark, turns measurable sound into an art direction, and preserves the real dog while painting it. Every bark becomes art.”
